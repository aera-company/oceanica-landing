## 9. Weaknesses in hierarchy and storytelling (opinion)

1. **It is an investor-relations template stretched into a corporate site.** The home meta description is literally "Relações com Investidores".
2. **The strongest claims are buried.** "0 to 3000 metres", "56 ROVs", "largest contingency operator in the country" and 24-hour response sit inside long tab paragraphs. The counters come last.
3. **The hero says nothing and does nothing.** A static slide with the tagline. No proof, no motion, no route.
4. **The best overview is hidden.** The 14-point field scene, the only place where the whole operation is visible at once, sits mid-page under "A Oceânica".
5. **The story stops in 2021,** just before the company more than doubled its Work Class capability.
6. **Dead ends.** Empty Cases, Awards and Diverless pages, "Nenhuma informação adicionada" on tooling, public test posts.
7. **Structure follows internal vocabulary** (Prevenção, CRD) and not the client's problem (keep the asset safe, respond when it fails, build what is missing).
8. **No proof layer.** No clients beyond one name, no cases, no safety metrics.
9. **Contact, legal and ethics are missing from the footer.**
10. **The company's real visual assets are unused:** green hulls on blue water, yellow ROVs in black water. The site shows stock.

## 10. What this means for the new design

- The depth concept is not a metaphor laid on top. **"0 to 3000 metres" is the company's own headline claim.** The descent in the hero ends at exactly that number.
- Three lines map cleanly to three chapters: Prevention, Contingency, Engineering.
- Verified display numbers available today: 0 to 3000 m · 56 ROVs · +2,300 people · 48 years · 1978 · 11 named vessels · 13 diving systems · 24 h response. The diver count needs a decision first.
- ESG can be told as the business itself: the mission is literally to prevent environmental accidents. It does not need a separate card.
- A request list for Oceânica is the next unlock: original ROV dive footage, vessel video, SVG logo, safety statistics, client and case permissions, the correct diver count.
