## Direction 1 · The operation in real size (pairs with A)

One sticky stage, about 3 viewports, the sea from above as the ground.

1. **56.** The 56 ROVs drawn as 56 small top-view silhouettes at the same scale as the vessel in the photograph. They arrange themselves on the blue in rows the size of a deck. The real count per model is not published, so they are drawn as one generic class and captioned "56 ROVs em 2026".
2. **2.300+.** The silhouettes give way to a dense field of 2,300 dots, each a person, forming the outline of an Oceânica vessel at true scale. A caption gives the proportion: "pessoas suficientes para tripular N embarcações", but only once Oceânica confirms crew sizes. Otherwise there is no comparison.
3. **48.** A horizontal timeline from 1978 to 2026. Each verified milestone (1978 start, 1988 subsea inspection, 2001 Macaé base and training centre, 2011 EPCista, 2017 SDSVs, 2019 Diverless, 2020 first Work Class contract, 2021 Work Class training centre, 2022–23 Work Class vessels) is a tick. The line dips deeper as the company's depth capability grew, from shallow ports and dams to 3,000 m. **Depth over time is the company's real story in one line.**

## Direction 2 · Numbers lit like equipment (pairs with B)

Darkness, one number at a time, each revealed by a pool of light as the vehicles were.
- **0 → 3000** is the first number. It is drawn as the water column itself, one vertical line, with the verified operating bands marked: divers to 50 m; observation ROVs to 1,000 to 1,500 m (Lynx, Surveyor); Work Class to 3,000 m (vessels) and 4,000 m (the vehicles' rating). This is the most informative graphic the company has and nobody shows it.
- **56 ROVs and 250+ pilots** follow as two numbers side by side. The relationship (more than four pilots per vehicle) says "operated around the clock" without a claim.
- **2.300+** ends the sequence as the largest number on screen, the only one in green.

## Direction 3 · The company on the map (pairs with C)

The field drawing zooms out to the Rio de Janeiro coastline: four verified bases (Rio de Janeiro, Macaé, Rio das Ostras, Niterói), the training centres (Rio das Ostras, Maricá), and an open sea that runs off the edge of the page.
- The figures attach to places: 2,300+ at the bases, 56 ROVs offshore, 48 years as the date on the map's title block.
- **Do not** draw oil fields, clients' platforms or vessel positions; none are published.

## What not to do
- No counters ticking up from zero on every scroll.
- No icons beside numbers.
- No figure without a source line.
- No "líder", "maior" or "única" in the numbers chapter unless it is quoted from the site with its exact scope. Example: "única empresa brasileira com contratos de ROV de 0 a 3000 m".
