## 5. Gaps that limit every concept

1. **No moving image of the operation.** The Lynx frame is a grab from a video, and the vessel stills come from a 2024 drone shoot, so footage almost certainly exists. It is the single most valuable request to Oceânica.
2. **No high-resolution subsea imagery.** Both underwater pictures are video grabs.
3. **No real diver imagery.** The only diver image is stock.
4. **No platform or FPSO imagery owned by Oceânica** beyond the two frames where they appear in the background. The infrastructure belongs to clients, so permission matters too.
5. **No vector logo, no brand guide.**
6. **No rights clearance** for any image.

## 6. Consequences for V05

- The hero has to prove scale with **relationships** (vessel next to FPSO, ROV on a deck that faces a production unit, the same team above and below the water). Single heroic objects cannot carry it.
- Where an image is missing, the frames use **drawing** (concept C) or **plain water with no equipment** (concept B). They never use invented footage of equipment or operations.
- Every frame is labelled as a study, and every generated element would be labelled as conceptual. No generated element exists yet.
